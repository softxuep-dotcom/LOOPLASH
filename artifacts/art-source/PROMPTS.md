# ImageGen source prompts

These are the prompts used for the first LOOPLASH runtime-art batch. Creature sheets were
generated on a chroma background, then converted to transparent masters and packaged by
`scripts/package-generated-art.py`.

## Meadow creatures

```text
Create a polished hand-painted 2D game creature sprite sheet on a perfectly flat solid
#00ff00 chroma-key background. Exact 2×2 equal-cell layout, one isolated creature centred
in each cell: top-left Puff, a round rosy cotton pod with cream eyes and seed tufts;
top-right Needler, an amber-gold floating thistle with a long needle snout and small fins;
bottom-left Shellbud, a coral-red six-petal armored flower creature with one golden knot;
bottom-right Bomb Bloom, a purple eight-petal fruit creature with a curled fuse and yellow
core. Plush botanical fantasy, bold readable silhouettes, soft cel shading, top-down 3/4
game view, readable at 48 px. No cast shadows, text, labels, borders, grid lines, UI, scenery,
particles, or extra objects. Keep generous clear green space around every creature.
```

## Reef creatures

```text
Using the meadow sheet as the exact style reference, create a polished hand-painted 2D game
creature sprite sheet on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background. Exact 2×2
equal-cell layout: top-left Skipper, a turquoise seed-manta; top-right Splitter, a sky-blue
jelly-coral with a central seam and twin lobes; bottom-left Mirrorling, a violet faceted
diamond crystal creature; bottom-right Bubble Ray, a dark blue manta with bubble pods and
exactly two golden armor knots. Plush botanical fantasy, bold readable silhouettes, soft cel
shading, top-down 3/4 game view, readable at 48 px. No cast shadows, text, labels, borders,
grid lines, UI, scenery, particles, or extra objects. Generous clear green space around each.
```

## Elites and boss

```text
Using the meadow and reef sheets as the exact style references, create a polished hand-painted
2D game creature sprite sheet on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background. Exact
2×2 equal-cell layout: top-left Knot Knight, a coral flower guardian with exactly three golden
knots; top-right Storm Spool, a cobalt-violet wound jelly spool with exactly two golden knots;
bottom-left Twin Maw, a turquoise triangular side-facing head designed to be mirrored, with
exactly two golden knots; bottom-right Tanglejaw, a large magenta and crimson boss with four
curled hook arms and exactly five golden knots. Plush botanical fantasy, bold readable
silhouettes, soft cel shading, top-down 3/4 game view, readable at small scale. No cast shadows,
text, labels, borders, grid lines, UI, scenery, particles, or extra objects. Generous clear
green space around each.
```

## Biome backgrounds

```text
Using the creature sheets as the style reference, create one top-down 2D game background image
with exactly two equal vertical panels and no dividing border. Left panel: Starlit Meadow, deep
navy ground with rosy cotton tufts and tiny warm seed lights concentrated around the outer
edges, leaving a calm low-contrast central play area. Right panel: Twilight Reef, deep ocean
navy ground with turquoise and coral fronds concentrated around the outer edges, leaving a
calm low-contrast central play area. Polished hand-painted botanical fantasy, soft cel-shaded
forms, seamless-feeling floor, no horizon. No creatures, characters, UI, text, labels, logos,
frames, or high-contrast objects in either centre.
```

## Player and needles

```text
Use the meadow creature sheet as a style reference only. Create one exact 2×2 equal-cell
top-down 2D player and weapon sprite sheet on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key
background. Top-left: Loomheart player anchor, a compact circular navy-and-cream magical
thread spool familiar with a bright golden star core and two small rosy leaf fins, heroic,
friendly, and symmetrical. Top-right: Dawn Needle, an elegant compact golden sewing shuttle
with a sharp triangular point, small sun gem, and cream thread detail, pointing exactly right.
Bottom-left: Twin Needle, a compact coral-pink double-pronged sewing shuttle with a central
rose gem and visibly two tips, pointing exactly right. Bottom-right: Moon Shuttle, a pale
icy-blue crescent sewing shuttle with a moon gem and one sharp tip, pointing exactly right.
Polished hand-painted plush botanical fantasy, warm highlights, bold silhouettes, soft cel
shading, top-down 3/4 game view, readable at 32–48 px. One isolated subject centred in each
cell with generous equal padding; all needles use comparable visible length. Uniform green
background only: no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor, green subject colors,
text, labels, borders, grid lines, UI, scenery, particles, extra objects, logos, or watermark.
```
